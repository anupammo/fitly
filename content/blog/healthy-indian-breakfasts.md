## What makes a breakfast filling

Protein and fibre keep you full longer than refined carbs. A breakfast with 15 g or more of protein usually keeps hunger away until lunch.

## Easy swaps

- Add peanuts and vegetables to poha, or have it with a glass of milk.
- Choose idli or plain dosa with sambar over fried vada.
- Make parathas with less oil and add a katori of curd.
- Add paneer or tofu crumble to chillas.
